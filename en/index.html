What Makes DREAM Different

The distinctive feature is not any single ingredient — higher-dimensional latent structure, projection, coarse-graining, stretched-exponential retention, and topology all have analogues elsewhere. It is the discipline imposed on the whole construction.

DREAM operates with a closed explanatory vocabulary. You start with the axioms and the small mathematical apparatus they authorize. Then, when you move from quantum mechanics to gravity to cosmology to biology to cognition to social systems, you do not grant yourself permission to invent a new DREAM-specific particle, field, force, coupling, hidden variable, dimensional sector, or tunable constant every time something becomes difficult.

You ask instead: What does the same lossy-projection machinery imply here?

\[ \underbrace{\text{Axioms},\;K_\lambda,\;\pi,\;R,\;\lambda_q,\;D,\;J_\pi,\;\text{topological invariants}}_{\text{fixed conceptual budget}} \;\longrightarrow\; \underbrace{\text{QFT, GR, cosmology, biology, cognition, }\ldots}_{\text{independent phenomena}} \]

DREAM does not replace local science. GR remains the correct effective description of gravitation. QFT remains the correct effective description of quantum fields. Evolution remains indispensable to biology. DREAM attempts a different level of explanation: why do all these effective worlds have the particular kinds of invariance, loss, locality, hierarchy, focusing, and finite recoverability that they do?

The central mathematical question: What structure survives when a richer state is represented through a finite, non-invertible observable map? The term informational retention is deliberately chosen — it says what S2 measures without prematurely claiming a microscopic physical mechanism.

Minimal-Extension Principle

DREAM explanations must be constructed from the existing axioms, derived projection mathematics, and empirically measured quantities. A new domain does not license the introduction of a new hidden entity, force, coupling, dimension, kernel form, or free parameter solely to obtain agreement. When the existing framework cannot account for an observation, the discrepancy is retained as a limitation or falsification pressure rather than repaired by ad hoc extension.

This is DREAM's deepest discipline: non-augmentation under explanatory pressure. A new phenomenon does not, by itself, license new fundamental machinery — the existing axioms must first be exhausted. It gives DREAM a severe failure criterion: if some major class of phenomena cannot be represented without adding an ad hoc DREAM-specific mechanism, that is evidence against the sufficiency of the framework. You don't get to save it simply by adding another invisible object.

The same economy applies to dimensionality: if 10D works, don't use 40. If the existing axioms work, don't invent new ones. If S2 works, don't invent a second decay mechanism for the awkward datasets. If the framework fails somewhere, record the failure.

The claim is not that DREAM has a huge toolbox capable of explaining almost anything. It is almost the inverse: the toolbox stays small while the territory keeps getting larger. If that continues to work empirically, that is the unusual result. The thing worth measuring is an explanatory compression ratio: how many independent phenomena are coherently constrained, divided by how many independent assumptions and free machinery are used. DREAM's claim becomes stronger if the numerator increases while the denominator stays essentially fixed.

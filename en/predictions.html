S2 — Retention Law Multidomain Survival (r² > 0.96 in 5 domains)

Claim. Interference-grade information decays with probe scale as a stretched exponential with kernel parameters \(\lambda_q\) and \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}\):

\[ \begin{aligned} R(\lambda) &= \exp\!\Big[-\big(\tfrac{\lambda}{\lambda_q}\big)^{D_{\mathrm{eff}}}\Big],\\ y(\lambda) &:= \ln(-\ln R) = D_{\mathrm{eff}}\ln\lambda - D_{\mathrm{eff}}\ln\lambda_q. \end{aligned}\]

Why this rating (updated 2026). The form fits five unrelated domains with r² > 0.96 in every case: SDSS (D=1.16, r²=0.999), DESI (D=1.06, r²=0.997), Bitcoin (D=0.38, r²=0.983), Ethereum (D=0.44, r²=0.974), Cognitive reaction times (D=0.37, r²=0.968). The form perseveres where standard pipelines (P(k)+filter) have no equivalent — for cognitive data, P(k)+filter has no natural formulation for ex-Gaussian reaction-time distributions, yet S2 fits cleanly. On real cosmological data (SDSS, DESI), S2 beats the exact P(k)+Gaussian-filter prediction by Δr² ≈ +0.09 to +0.14 — see S2 vs P(k)+Filter Test.

What was falsified (2026). The original derived claim that D and λ_q are invariant across probes was falsified. Five probes (Gaussian, Fourier, Box, Wavelet, PCA) on the same data give D values varying by factors of 2–10. The form is the foundation; the parameter values are probe-dependent. See F1.

How to test (any instrument)

  • Define a monotone smoother tied to resolution; compute \(R_{\mathrm{HF}}(\lambda)\) and/or \(R_{\mathrm{corr}^2}(\lambda)\).
  • Fit \(y=\ln(-\ln R)\) vs \(\ln\lambda\); extract \(\widehat{D}_{\mathrm{eff}},\,\widehat{\lambda}_q\). Report CI + λ-window.
  • Show cross-context collapse after rescaling by \(\widehat{\lambda}_q\).
  • 2026 addition: report D and λ_q as probe-dependent summaries, not invariants. Compare to P(k)+filter baseline explicitly.

Falsification. Persistent curvature in \(y(\lambda)\) after calibration across multiple platforms and domains. Not observed in 2026 testing across five domains.

T7.1 — Multi-Regime S2 (Piecewise Composition) Confirmed · 25 datasets · p=3.4×10⁻¹⁸

Claim. S2 is the local law of retention. The global retention curve is a piecewise composition of S2 regimes, with the kernel entering different regimes at reproducible fractional scale thresholds. The transition scale \(\lambda^\*\) clusters at fractional positions \([0.25, 0.38]\) of the t-range (IQR) across unrelated domains.

\[ R(\lambda)\;\approx\; \begin{cases} \exp\!\Big[-\Big(\tfrac{\lambda}{\lambda_{q,1}}\Big)^{D_1}\Big] & \lambda<\lambda^\*\\[4pt] A_2\,\exp\!\Big[-\Big(\tfrac{\lambda}{\lambda_{q,2}}\Big)^{D_2}\Big] & \lambda\ge\lambda^\* \end{cases} \]

Why this rating. All five falsifiable predictions confirmed on 25 real datasets (World Bank, Open-Meteo, USGS, Zenodo, CoinGecko, Binance, GitHub) covering 6 domains: financial, environmental, geophysical, crypto, scouting, ecological. 100% show strong piecewise improvement (ΔAICc > 4, median ΔAICc = 92.6, max = 401.2). Binomial p = 3.4×10⁻¹⁸ vs single-S2 null. Positive control passes (98% recovery of known 2-regime t_break within 0.1).

Five predictions, all confirmed

  1. P1: Piecewise strongly beats single-S2 where the kernel crosses a regime boundary (ΔAICc > 4). 25/25 datasets (100%).
  2. P2: Transitions cluster at reproducible fractional positions across unrelated domains (std < 0.20). Real std = 0.169, IQR = [0.251, 0.375].
  3. P3: D systematically increases from regime 1 to regime 2 (D₂ > D₁ in >80% of cases). Observed: 22/25 (88%), median D₁=1.06 → D₂=5.08.
  4. P4: Null-model control: pure single-S2 synthetic curves should NOT show this pattern above chance. Observed: only 20% strong improvement on null vs 100% on real; binomial p = 3.4×10⁻¹⁸.
  5. P5: Positive control: pipeline recovers known 2-regime t_break in >80% of synthetic 2-regime cases. Observed: 98% within 0.1 of true value.

How to test (any retention curve)

  • Fit single S2: \(R(\lambda) = \exp[-(\lambda/\lambda_q)^D]\). Record AICc_single.
  • Fit piecewise S2 with searched breakpoint \(\lambda^\*\) (try 5-7 candidates spanning 25%-75% of t-range). Record AICc_piecewise and optimal \(\lambda^\*\).
  • Compute ΔAICc = AICc_single − AICc_piecewise. If ΔAICc > 4 → strong evidence for multi-regime.
  • Report the regime pair (D₁, λ_{q,1}, D₂, λ_{q,2}) and the transition fraction \(\lambda^\*/(\lambda_{\max}-\lambda_{\min})\).
  • Flag entries where D₂ hits the upper bound (D=10) — these may need a different second-regime functional form.

Falsification. If piecewise-S2 does NOT strongly beat single-S2 on real curves (ΔAICc < 4 in <50% of cases), or if transition positions do NOT cluster (std > 0.20 in fractional coordinates), T7.1 is falsified. Both controls (null and positive) must pass for the pipeline to be valid.

See Retention page T7.1, Theorems T7.1, and tests registry for the full evidence chain. All data publicly fetched, no synthetic in the real-data column.

S3 — Cosmology & Fractal Spectrum Partially confirmed (strong support to S2)

Claim. The effective spectral exponent \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}\) governs retained-mode counts and the density of states; structure exhibits bounded fractality below the coherence cliff and flows toward homogeneity above it.

\[ \begin{aligned} N(\Omega) &\propto \Omega^{D_{\mathrm{eff}}},\\ \rho(\Omega) &= \frac{dN}{d\Omega} \propto \Omega^{D_{\mathrm{eff}}-1},\\ D_{\mathrm{eff}}(\lambda) &\nearrow 3 \quad \text{as } \lambda \to \infty. \end{aligned} \]

Why this rating. Survey-style trends (multiscale analyses, web-to-homogeneity behavior) and spectrum-page diagnostics align with S2’s prediction of bounded fractality and the flow to \(D\!\to\!3\) on large scales. We treat this as strong support for S2 but keep the rating “partial” pending harmonized, pre-registered pipelines across DESI/Euclid/LSST.

Program

  • Exponents: fit \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}\) from retained-mode counting / spectral slopes across band-limited layers.
  • Flow check: verify approach to homogeneity (counts \(\sim r^3\), correlation decay) above the cliff.
  • Cross-checks: compare cosmology-inferred \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}\) to lab-inferred from S2 fits (same transform).

Falsification. Stable inconsistency between cosmology-inferred and lab-inferred \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}\); absence of homogeneity flow at large scales after systematics.

S5 — Strong Lensing Invariants Partially confirmed (early)

Claim. Under angular smoothing \( \lambda \), two retention channels behave as S2 predicts with a shared \(\lambda_q\): rapid HF-power loss vs. slower structural-correlation decay; the ring/arc skeleton (critical-set connectivity/parity) persists on CPI ridges as HF texture collapses.

\[ \ln(-\ln R(\lambda))=D_{\mathrm{eff}}\ln\lambda-D_{\mathrm{eff}}\ln\lambda_q,\quad R_{\mathrm{HF}}(\lambda)\prec R_{\mathrm{corr}^2}(\lambda). \]

Status. Early analyses show the two-channel split and approximate log-linear fingerprints consistent with a common \(\lambda_q\); broader, harmonized reductions are in flight.

Program

  • VIS/NIR/mm cross-band λ-grid; fit both channels; show common \(\widehat{\lambda}_q\) and slopes.
  • Summarize critical-set topology vs. \(\lambda\); map CPI ridges and their persistence.

Falsification. Divergent \(\widehat{\lambda}_q\) across bands for the same lens; lack of two-channel split; no CPI persistence signature.

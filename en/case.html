Case for D.R.E.A.M.

A single projection with a single retention law — delivering ontology, parsimony, and near-term tests.

D.R.E.A.M. is a mathematics of informational retention under lossy projection. It models our 4-D universe as a finite-resolution projection of a compact Meta-Manifold via a fixed, non-invertible kernel \(K_{\lambda}\). The central mathematical question is:

What structure survives when a richer state is represented through a finite, non-invertible observable map?

If \(\Phi \mapsto K_\lambda[\Phi]\) is lossy, then the accessible world is necessarily characterized by survival and disappearance of distinctions. You can then ask about the retained fraction \(R(\lambda)\), the characteristic retention scale \(\lambda_q\), the shape of the loss \(D\), persistent ridges and hubs, degeneracies of preimages, topological quantities that survive the map, and structures that disappear beneath resolution. Those are all variations on one question: What survives the projection?

The intellectual discipline is not merely "keep the theory simple." It is stronger: define the primitive rules once, then live with their consequences. You start with a small set of primitives — source space, non-invertible projection, finite resolution, retention, locality/topological constraints — and then everything downstream must be generated from those primitives.

\[ \text{projection axioms} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{retention mathematics} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{structural consequences} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{domain-specific manifestations} \]

Physics, biology, cognition, networks, economics — these are not supposed to acquire separate DREAM laws. They become different places in which the consequences of the same projection mathematics can be examined.

Non-Augmentation Under Explanatory Pressure

DREAM's deepest discipline is non-augmentation under explanatory pressure. This is not "DREAM will never change" — a scientific framework must be corrigible. It is:

A new phenomenon does not, by itself, license new fundamental machinery. The existing axioms must first be exhausted.

Formally, the aspiration is a small generative rule set \(\mathcal{A} = \{A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n\}\) followed by a deductive closure \(\operatorname{Cn}(\mathcal{A}) = \{P : \mathcal{A} \vdash P\}\). When a new domain appears, DREAM does not change \(\mathcal{A}\) because the phenomenon is inconvenient. It asks whether that phenomenon can be represented somewhere inside the consequences of the same starting structure.

When \(\mathcal{A} \nRightarrow P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), there are only a few intellectually disciplined possibilities: the proposed derivation is wrong, the phenomenon was incorrectly mapped to DREAM variables, the empirical claim is wrong, or the framework is incomplete. What you don't permit is:

\[ \mathcal{A} + A_{\text{convenient}} \Rightarrow P_{\mathrm{obs}} \qquad \text{(forbidden)} \]

The evidential argument is about compression: a small generative rule set produces a large diversity of independently observed structure.

\[ \underbrace{\text{small generative rule set}}_{\mathcal{A}} \;\longrightarrow\; \underbrace{\{P_1, P_2, \ldots, P_m\}}_{\text{independently observed properties}} \]

The claim is not that DREAM has a huge toolbox capable of explaining almost anything. It is almost the inverse: the toolbox stays small while the territory keeps getting larger. If that continues to work empirically, that is the unusual result.

Derived Mathematics vs. Empirical Identification

DREAM distinguishes two epistemic stages:

  1. Derived mathematics: \(\mathcal{A} \Rightarrow P\). A certain class of projections generates property \(P\). This is a theorem, not an empirical claim.
  2. Empirical identification: \(\text{data} \approx P\). Some real system exhibits property \(P\). This is an observation, not a proof of the ontology.

What becomes interesting is when numerous independently known properties — \(P_1, P_2, \ldots, P_m\) — keep landing inside the consequences of the same tiny starting set: \(\mathcal{A} \Rightarrow \{P_1, P_2, \ldots, P_m\}\). Then the evidential argument is increasingly about compression: small generative rule set → large diversity of independently observed structure.

The term informational retention is deliberately chosen because it says what S2 is doing — measuring what survives lossy projection — without prematurely claiming a microscopic physical mechanism.

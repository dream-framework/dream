Test 15 — Real-Data Hologram Test (R-ratio + lattice αs + LEP αW)

Sharper version of Test 14: uses REAL experimental R(s) from e+e- → hadrons (PDG 2024 compilation) to compute Im[α_em]. Im[α_em]/Re[α_em] ratio is ~5-10% in the 1-10 GeV range (vs 0.1% in crude 1-loop model), making the test much sharper.
VERDICT: AMBIGUOUS
Best alignment at Q* = 161.4 GeV has max pairwise phase difference of 0.100 rad (5.7°). The null control shows this is MARGINAL — in the 5th percentile of 1000 random trials (null best = 0.92°, 5th pct = 14.56°). Even with real R(s) data providing larger Im[α_em], the alignment remains statistically marginal. The hologram hypothesis is suggestive but unsupported.

Method: Real-data complexification uses the dispersion relation Im[α_em(s)] = α_em(0) × R(s)/(12π) / (1 - Δα_lept - Re[Π_had])² where R(s) = σ(e+e- → hadrons)/σ(e+e- → μ+μ-) is from PDG 2024 compilation (BESIII, CMD-3, SND, BaBar, KLOE). Leptonic running Δα_lept(s) is exact analytic formula. Re[Π_had] is log-parametrized to Δα_had(m_Z) = 0.0276 (PDG 2024). α_s(Q) uses 1+2-loop running with Shirkov-Solovtsov analytic Im[α_s] (small perturbatively). α_W(Q) uses 1-loop SU(2) running with W self-energy Im[α_W] above 2m_W = 160 GeV. POSITIVE threshold: max |Δφ| < 6° AND beats null. NEGATIVE threshold: max |Δφ| > 90° OR trivial. Sources: PDG 2024, Deur et al. 2016 (QCD), Alekseev 2002 (analytic coupling).

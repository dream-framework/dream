Meta-S2: Recursive Retention

Эмпирическое наблюдение

Across the current dataset of 74 measured systems — spanning cognitive decay, financial markets, cosmological structure, quantum decoherence, environmental time series, and others — each system was fitted individually to the S2 retention law:

$$R(\lambda) = \exp\!\left[-\left(\frac{\lambda}{\lambda_q}\right)^{D_{\mathrm{eff}}}\right]$$

This produced 74 independent estimates of D_eff. The distribution of those D_eff values was then analyzed.

Находка 1: Распределение D следует S2

The complementary cumulative distribution of D_eff — that is, the fraction of systems with D ≥ d, as a function of d — was fitted to the S2 functional form:

$$P(D \ge d) = \exp\!\left[-\left(\frac{d}{\lambda_q^{\mathrm{meta}}}\right)^{D_{\mathrm{meta}}}\right]$$

Result:

  • D_meta = 1.611
  • λ_qmeta = 14.36
  • R² = 0.978

This means: the S2 retention law, which describes how individual systems lose information with probe scale, also describes how the collection of fitted exponents is distributed across systems. The ensemble of S2 parameters itself follows S2.

For comparison, a power-law fit to the same distribution gave P(D ≥ d) ~ d−1.015 with R² = 0.755 — a weaker fit than S2.

Находка 2: Бимодальность распределения

Three tests were applied to check whether the distribution of D values is unimodal or multimodal:

The Gaussian Mixture Model identifies two clusters: a large component centered at D ≈ 1.12 (91.9% of systems) and a smaller component at D ≈ 4.47 (8.1% of systems). The gap between these clusters is statistically significant (ΔBIC = −38.1, strongly favoring 2 components).

Note: The bimodality is not simply at the D = 1 threshold. The two clusters are "typical systems" (D ≈ 1.1) and "extreme extractors" (D ≈ 4.5), not "natural" vs "extraction." This is a different and perhaps more interesting structure than initially expected.

Находка 3: Смещение выборки

This is not a random sample. The 74 systems were selected because S2 fitting was attempted on them. The sample is biased toward:

  • Systems with available downloadable data (FRED, World Bank, USGS, Zenodo)
  • Systems where stretched-exponential decay was plausible
  • Domains where the DREAM framework predicted a specific D regime

The claim "65% of systems have D > 1" applies to this sample, not to all measurable phenomena in the universe. A truly random sample might show a different distribution.

Гипотеза (Мета-S2)

What follows is a hypothesis, not a theorem. It is motivated by the empirical observations above but has not been proven. It is offered as a direction for future investigation.

Рекурсивное свойство

The strongest empirical finding is not philosophical — it is mathematical:

S2 describes individual system retention. The distribution of S2 exponents across systems also follows S2.

This recursive property — S2 describing the distribution of S2 parameters — is genuinely unusual. If it persists as additional systems are measured, it may indicate that S2 is a fixed point under aggregation: collections of systems possess emergent retention dynamics governed by the same functional form as their constituents.

This would be analogous to how the normal distribution is its own conjugate prior in Bayesian inference, or how stable distributions are closed under convolution. S2 would be closed under a different operation — the operation of "collecting independent retention processes and asking how their exponents are distributed."

Что бы это значило

If Meta-S2 is real:

  • S2 is not just a curve that fits many datasets. It is a distribution that is self-similar under aggregation. The law governs both the parts and the whole.
  • The meta-exponent D_meta ≈ 1.6 has meaning. It describes the "retention of retention" — how quickly the property of having a particular D_eff itself degrades as you move across the space of systems.
  • The bimodality may indicate two classes of systems. The GMM clusters at D ≈ 1.1 and D ≈ 4.5 suggest there may be two qualitatively different regimes — not "natural vs extraction" but "ordinary vs extreme." What distinguishes the extreme cluster is an open question.

Что это ПОКА не значит

  • "The universe is in extraction regime." This is too strong. The current sample is biased. We can say "within the current dataset, the majority of measured systems have D > 1," but we cannot generalize to the universe without a random sample.
  • "Extraction cascades upward." This is a proposed mechanism, not an established consequence. It would require either a theoretical derivation (showing that nested S2 processes compose to give max(D_i)) or observational evidence on hierarchical systems. Neither has been provided yet.
  • "D = 1 is a phase transition." The bimodality tests show two clusters, but they are at D ≈ 1.1 and D ≈ 4.5, not at D = 1. The threshold D = 1 separates natural from extraction within individual systems, but the meta-distribution does not clearly show a gap there.

Как это проверить

The hypothesis makes a testable prediction:

As the test registry grows beyond 74 systems, the meta-S2 fit should remain stable (D_meta ≈ 1.6, R² > 0.95) if the recursive property is real. If D_meta drifts significantly or R² drops below 0.9 as more systems are added, the recursive property was an artifact of the current sample.

Specifically:

  • If the next 100 systems (doubling the registry) still fit S2 with D_meta ≈ 1.5–1.7, the hypothesis is strengthened.
  • If the fit degrades, the current result was selection bias.
  • If the bimodality persists with the same cluster locations, the two-class structure is real.
  • If the bimodality disappears, it was a small-sample artifact.

Метод

How the meta-fit was constructed

Data: 74 D_eff values extracted from the DREAM test registry (en/tests.html) as of 2026-07-28. Each D_eff was individually fitted to R(λ) = exp[−(λ/λ_q)^D] using nonlinear least squares (scipy.optimize.curve_fit) on either autocorrelation functions (for time series) or magnitude-frequency retention curves (for event data).

Meta-fit: The 74 D values were sorted. The complementary CDF P(D ≥ d) was computed at 50 evenly-spaced thresholds d ∈ [0.1, 5.0]. This CDF was then fitted to the S2 form P(D ≥ d) = exp[−(t/λ_q)^D] where t is the rank index (1 to 50).

Bimodality tests:

  • Bimodality coefficient: BC = (skewness² + 1) / kurtosis. Threshold for bimodality: BC > 0.555.
  • Gaussian Mixture Model: 1-component vs 2-component, compared via BIC and AIC. Lower BIC preferred.

Software: Python 3.12, scipy 1.x, scikit-learn (GaussianMixture).

Limitations:

  • Hartigan's dip test was not available in the installed scipy version; the bimodality coefficient was used as a substitute. A proper dip test should be run.
  • The meta-fit uses rank as the independent variable, not D itself. A direct fit to the empirical CDF would be more rigorous.
  • The 74 systems are not independent — some share datasets (e.g., multiple FRED series from the same economic system).
  • Several D values hit the fitting boundary at D = 5.0 (the upper limit of the optimizer), which may distort the tail of the distribution.

Analysis performed 2026-07-28. Data: DREAM Test Registry (74 systems with fitted D_eff).
